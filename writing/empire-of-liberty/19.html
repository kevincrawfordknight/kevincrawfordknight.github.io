Empire of Liberty

19. Your progress is astounding

“That was weird,” I told Brenda later.

“What was weird?” she asked.

“You told Gerald Yang that you and I work together on problems in algorithmic number theory.”

“Yes.”

“But we don’t work on algorithmic number theory. We work on algebraic number theory.”

Brenda studied me, her lips scrunching inward like she wanted to say something.

“It’s a joke,” I said.

“Good Lord!” she said.

“You said to tell jokes.”

“Your progress is astounding,” she said.

She asked me how the small talk was going.

“I’m not even sure what small talk is anymore.”

“Short temporary conversation,” she said. “Why don’t we try it right now?”

“Okay,” I said.

I remembered to make eye contact.

“You can ask me something simple,” Brenda said.

“Okay, which Canada are you from?” I asked.

In high school, we learned there were three: a Red one, a White one, and a French one.

“Very nice question,” she said. “Actually, I grew up in Los Angeles.”

The capital of California, I vaguely recalled.

“It's the capital of California now,” said Brenda, “but it wasn’t back then.”

It almost felt like she could read my mind, maybe because we’d spent so much time together. Or maybe that was just how normal conversation worked, between friends.

“This is how normal conversation works,” she said. “You’re doing great.”

“Small talk,” I said.
