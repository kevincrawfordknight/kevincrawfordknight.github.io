Empire of Liberty

7. Anarchy

I had two problems.

One was how to get out of the current mess in Cherry Xu’s office.

For that, I had the Projector. But it wasn’t good to use the Projector, and besides, it didn’t always work. And I’d vowed not to use it on friends and classmates. And also, there were side effects.

The other problem was that I had no plan for life outside of Alicent. HR issued a letter of support for my apartment rental, so if I lost my job, I’d lose my apartment and my ration coupons.

What I had was a pipe dream to move to California and live with my boyfriend, a mathematician named Carson Hawke. He wasn’t my boyfriend, exactly, but we’d exchanged a long series of audio messages over the Strollnet. Carson Hawke invented the Strollnet, if you can believe that. Sunny Li said Carson Hawke was imaginary, like the square root of negative one. But he sent me a non-imaginary mixtape, and I sent him my software for digital money.

It was a pipe dream because California was a swamp of drugs and anarchy, where the people’s Governor was a woman who made the type of films I don’t know the English word for. You didn’t need a passport to get into the lawless breakaway region, but you needed a paperwork to get out of China, and the Ministry of Defense rarely issued that.

Brenda could get me out, or so she’d claimed that one night while high on white stimulant powder.

But those days, Brenda was nowhere to be found.
