Empire of Liberty

3. This ring was a gift

Oslo, 1976.

I am an instructor at the Kolmogorov Institute of Mathematics at the University of Oslo in Norway.

The name on my office door is fake, because I am in hiding.

I can’t remember my real name or even the reason I’m hiding.

Because I have an Asian face, I can lie to my colleagues and say that I come from the Soviet East. Fortunately, few Norwegians ask questions, especially in winter.

My memories may be gone, but my mathematical faculties are intact. I have just completed a private proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, a classic problem in prime number theory, unsolved since 1859. The more I work on the proof, the simpler it gets, which is always a good sign.

I check my mood ring. Bluish-green: I am relaxed and aware of my surroundings. This ring was a gift, I somehow remember, so I open my math notebook and write this new fact down, along with three bullet points.
