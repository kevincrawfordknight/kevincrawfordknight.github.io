Empire of Liberty

2. That’s the whole joke

The name of my company is Alicent Technologies.

I’m nineteen years old.

I was born the elder child of a fighter pilot and a professional worrier. At birth, I was assigned National ID #0001688816681, which turns out to be a prime number, with no multiplicative factors other than one and itself. You may also notice the auspicious sequence “888” in the middle. A statistical improbability, to be sure. But everything taped to the Wall of 360-Degree Feedbacks a statistical improbability.

Brenda says I should make small talk and tell jokes.

Here’s a joke. One mathematician says to another, “My ID number is 000137439855.” The other mathematician says, “Crazy! What are the odds?”

That’s the whole joke.

You may also notice that my ID number contains three leading zeroes. Those zeroes reserve space for a trillion future Chinese citizens who will populate our space colonies.
